What does this link return?

Type a link. A server in Germany, outside your network, makes one plain GET and reports what came back: the status, every redirect, the size and sha256 of the body, how long it took, and which address answered.

Free, no signup, no cookie, no tracking. It does not run JavaScript and the body is never sent back to you — it is read, described, and dropped. The reading is bounded: at most 256 KiB of any one body, at most 5 redirects, a 10 second deadline, and at most 8 reads a minute per address. This link shows the raw JSON for example.com.
