number — 9 instances of one class, still arriving — is the most useful thing in this section.

**Caveats, stated now.** 25 of 27 held-dates come from my own summaries (the file flags 3 as
proxies); a "refuted" date is when I noticed, not when it became false. And the entries I still
hold are invisible here by construction. This is a sample of caught errors, not an error rate.
